        Entities — and who actually pays

        The same defendant often shows up in multiple states' lawsuits. This links them across jurisdictions instead of leaving each filing siloed. Click a defendant for two things: every action they're named in, and a real, sourced corporate-structure note — because a judgment against a familiar brand name doesn't tell you which legal entity's balance sheet actually absorbs it. DuPont's liability is contractually split across three separate companies from a 2015 spinoff; Honeywell's is an open question after its 2025 Solstice spinoff that we checked directly against the actual separation agreement. Entries too vague to identify a real company (e.g. "gear makers, not itemized") are excluded and counted separately, not guessed at.
