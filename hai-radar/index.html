What this actually is, and its real limits

Stated honestly, same discipline as every other tool on this site:

  • CMS Health Deficiencies field correction, found live before building this: CMS's own infection_control_inspection_deficiency flag does NOT mean "this citation is about infection control" -- a real F0880 infection-control citation at Immediate-Jeopardy severity had that flag set to "N". This tool filters on the real, meaningful fields (deficiency_tag_number == "0880" and scope_severity_code) instead.
  • CMS HAI SIR: only surfaces measures explicitly flagged "Worse than the National Benchmark" -- a real, CMS-computed comparison, not a threshold we invented.
  • Matching is name-based -- a facility operating under a different DBA than the one on your submission will not match.
  • A finding is a real federal citation or infection-rate measure under this exact name, not a legal conclusion about the standard of care.
