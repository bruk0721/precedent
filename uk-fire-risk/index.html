Real MHCLG data — England, by Fire and Rescue Authority

Which UK fire brigades carry more injury risk than the rest?

Built from official MHCLG fire statistics (FIRE0102 and FIRE0508), not a US-style port of the Firefighter Presumption Pricing Engine. The UK has no statutory firefighter cancer presumption law to price against — so instead of a presumption engine, this is a straight, real benchmark: reported personnel injuries per 1,000 incidents attended, by authority, checked against both the England-wide rate and that authority's own 5-year trend.

Fire & Rescue Authority injury-rate benchmark

Why this isn't a presumption-pricing engine

The US product works because 20+ US states have passed laws that legally presume certain cancers are job-caused for firefighters, which is a real, date-stamped pricing trigger. The UK's Industrial Injuries Advisory Council has explicitly declined to add cancer (other than mesothelioma) to the prescribed-disease list for firefighters, and the Fire Brigades Union is still actively campaigning for one. Until that changes, there's no statutory exposure to price in the UK the way the US engine does — so this tool measures something real and different (reported injury rate vs. benchmark) rather than quietly reusing a US framing that wouldn't survive a basic coverage-counsel question.
