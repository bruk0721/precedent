PFAS casualty exposure

Where does this corporate family actually touch PFAS, today?

Not a litigation-risk score — a real, live cross-match of a company's own SEC-filed subsidiary tree against EPA's real PFAS Analytic Tools data: who in the family reported manufacturing or importing a real PFAS chemical under TSCA, and who reported releasing one on-site under TRI. Extends the Console's accumulation screen into the single risk category named most often as systemic.

What this actually is, and its real limits

Stated honestly, same discipline as every other tool on this site:

  • Not a litigation-risk score. This does not compete with scientific-literature-mining emerging-risk scores (the kind Praedicat/Moody's builds) -- it answers a narrower, complementary question: where does this specific corporate family have real, reported PFAS manufacture, import, or release today, by named facility.
  • TRI reporting has real thresholds. Only facilities above chemical-specific volume thresholds, in covered NAICS sectors, have to report at all -- a real PFAS user below that threshold, or outside a covered sector, won't show up here even if it genuinely uses or discharges PFAS.
  • TSCA Chemical Data Reporting only covers manufacturers and importers, not downstream formulators or users of PFAS-containing inputs bought from someone else.
  • Matching is name-based against each subsidiary's own Exhibit-21 legal name -- a subsidiary trading under a different DBA/trade name will not match, in either direction.
  • Not yet built, disclosed rather than hidden: EPA CompTox/CTX's chemical-hazard scoring and UCMR 5's drinking-water occurrence data were both named in the research behind this tool, but need separate join logic this first build doesn't have yet.
  • SEC Exhibit 21 only covers SEC filers -- a privately held insured's corporate family is out of scope entirely, same as the Accumulation Screen.
