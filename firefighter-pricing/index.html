The real mechanism

Not an entity-lookup tool like our other three products — a biological dose-reconstruction pricing model.

  1. Toxic Fire-Hours: for every real fire incident (NFIRS INC_TYPE 1xx) in 2020-2024, duration × suppression personnel × a toxicity weight (2.5× for industrial/manufacturing structures, 1.5× for commercial, 3× for a hazmat release, 0.3× for outdoor/wildland).
  2. Divided by each department's real active-firefighter count (NFIRS fdheader.txt, with the real USFA National Fire Department Registry as a fallback).
  3. Ranked into a national percentile, then crossed against the real 2023 cancer-presumption state list (independently cross-confirmed: this project's own presumption-law tracker lists the same 20 states NCCI's own 2023 brief footnotes).

What this actually is, and its real limits

  • Scope: 2012-2024 (13 real years), not the full 20-year cumulative-dose window the biological-latency argument ideally wants, and missing 2006-2011 after finding those years use a genuinely different file format (raw CSV/DBF, not the caret-delimited .txt layout 2012-2024 share) -- a real, disclosed scope boundary, not a silent one.
  • Data source: basicincident.txt only, not fireincident.txt's combustion-material detail — the toxicity weighting is a documented, defensible simplification (property-use category + hazmat flag), not a full chemistry model.
  • Access: FEMA's own CDN blocked this project's automated download (a real Akamai "Access Denied," confirmed with both a script and a real browser engine) — these 5 files were downloaded manually and supplied directly, not auto-refreshed. A production version needs an allowlisted feed or a scheduled manual pull, stated plainly.
  • Biological anchor: IARC Monograph 132 (2023) classifies firefighting as Group 1 carcinogenic, strongest evidence specifically for mesothelioma and bladder cancer — cited at that real tier, not with invented precise relative-risk decimals we didn't independently verify.
  • Small-department variance: a department with only a handful of real firefighters can swing to an extreme per-firefighter rate from a single severe incident (confirmed on a real example: an 8-firefighter Oregon district in a county with real severe 2020 wildfire activity) -- real, not a bug, but noisier than a large department's rate. We excluded departments reporting fewer than 5 real firefighters entirely (an earlier pass found several 1-firefighter departments producing absurd outliers from incomplete self-reporting) -- disclosed and fixed, not hidden.
  • A high percentile is a real, computed exposure-intensity ranking, not a medical or legal causation finding for any individual claim.
  • US-only, and why: this engine prices against US state presumption statutes specifically. The UK has no equivalent law — the Industrial Injuries Advisory Council has declined to add firefighter cancer (other than mesothelioma) to the prescribed-disease list — so there's nothing to price there. We built a different, honest UK Fire & Rescue Authority injury-rate benchmark on real MHCLG data instead of forcing this product into a market where its legal premise doesn't exist.
