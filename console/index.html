Entity traversal — veil-piercing signal

Flags a docket where a complaint names BOTH a known subsidiary and its parent company as defendants — the moment a mass-tort case stops naming just the subsidiary and reaches up to the parent's much larger corporate insurance tower.

Facility-level chemical reporting (EPA TRI)

▸

Real EPA Toxics Release Inventory facility reports flagged as PFAS compounds, matched by EPA's own standardized parent-company name — not a corporate-level disclosure, a specific manufacturing site and chemical.

Facility compliance history (EPA ECHO)

▸

Real EPA enforcement/compliance records for these same facilities, via ECHO's public API — a different signal than TRI's chemical-release reporting: has this specific site actually been cited, and for how much. Multiple registry entries can exist for one physical site (different permits/programs); every real record found is shown, not collapsed into one number.

Material event filings (SEC 8-K)

▸

Real-time signal, not an annual one: an 8-K is filed within 4 business days of a material event (a new settlement, consent decree, or material charge) — checked against the most recent 20 filings per curated company, so a new disclosure shows up here long before the next 10-K cycle would catch it.
