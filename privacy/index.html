Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [Company Legal Name] ("Nubuye," "we," "us") handles information in connection with the Nubuye website and products (the "Service").

1. What We Don't Collect

The interactive checks on this site (Claim Watchlist, Disclosure Auditor, Hospital Infection Radar, Firefighter Pricing) run entirely in your browser against a public data snapshot. What you type into those checks is never sent to our servers, stored, or logged. We have no record of what you searched.

2. What We Do Collect

If you contact us directly (for example, by email), we retain the information you choose to send us, for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and resolve that inquiry.

Like most websites, our hosting and content-delivery providers may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) for security and operational purposes. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

3. API Use

If you or your organization integrate with a Nubuye API directly (for example, a batch or subscription endpoint), the inputs you submit (such as a company name, facility name, or claim reference) are processed to generate a response and are not persisted beyond what is operationally necessary to do so, consistent with the specific API's own documentation.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use minimal local browser storage (such as remembering a light/dark theme preference) that stays on your own device and is never transmitted to us. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Third-Party Data Sources

The Service displays data from public government sources (CPSC, openFDA, NHTSA, EPA, OSHA, USAspending, CMS, NFIRS, and others). Each source's own privacy and usage terms govern the underlying data; Nubuye does not control how those agencies collect or publish it.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any information you do share with us directly. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use by insurance and risk professionals and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to hello@nubuye.com.
